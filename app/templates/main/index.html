What this system does

Built to address fake certificate problems in academic hiring

Can't be faked

Each certificate gets a unique hash. Change even one character and the hash won't match — it shows up as invalid immediately.

Fast verification

Employers paste a certificate ID or scan a QR code. Result comes up right away — no waiting, no calls, no paperwork.

Blockchain-backed

Records are mined into a blockchain with proof-of-work. Nobody — not even the admin — can silently change them later.

Easy sharing

Students get a QR code per certificate. Put it on a resume, share on WhatsApp — whoever scans it can verify it on the spot.

How it works

1
University issues it

The university fills in the student's details and clicks Issue. The system hashes the data and records a new block.

2
Block is saved

The certificate hash and transaction info get stored permanently. Browse them anytime in the blockchain explorer.

3
Student receives it

Certificate appears in the student's dashboard with a QR code. Download it or share the verification link directly.

4
Employer checks it

Scan the QR or type the certificate ID. The system checks the chain and returns whether it's valid or has been revoked.
